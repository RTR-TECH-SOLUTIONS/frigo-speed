Refrigerated transport

One pallet, 17 or 33? Refrigerated transport is for goods that must not warm up, freeze or wait in the heat at a loading dock. We collect them from you at the required temperature and hand them over the same way.

We run refrigerated semi-trailers for full loads, dual-temperature trailers and trailers with meat hooks, plus groupage space for smaller loads, in Romania and on international routes. When our trucks are busy, our forwarding desk finds a vetted carrier for your trip, on the same terms.

Types of refrigerated transport

Dual-temperature refrigerated transport

A movable bulkhead splits the trailer into two compartments, each with its own temperature. Frozen and chilled goods travel on the same trip, for example ice cream at −20 °C and dairy at +4 °C, so you do not pay for two trucks.

  • Two independent zones, between −25 °C and +25 °C
  • The bulkhead moves to fit the pallets in each zone
  • Each zone’s temperature logged separately

Groupage refrigerated transport

One pallet, five or fifteen? You pay only for the space you use. We combine several loads on the same route and deliver each one to its consignee, chilled or frozen, in standard or express transit.

  • From a single euro pallet to a full truck
  • Regular Romania – Italy routes, outbound and return
  • Chilled or frozen goods

Hanging meat transport on hooks

For chilled hanging meat we use trailers with rails and hooks fitted to the roof. Carcasses hang freely, do not touch each other and keep their shape on the road, while cold air flows around every piece.

  • Pork, beef, lamb and mutton carcasses, hams
  • Trailer washed and sanitised before every loading
  • Between −2 °C and +4 °C for the whole trip

Why ship with us

Good refrigerated transport means the right temperature, loading times kept and documents in order. We check all three on every trip.

  • The temperature you set

    The reefer unit holds the temperature set at loading, from −25 °C to +25 °C, and the readings are logged for the whole trip.

  • ATP-certified trailers

    Perishable goods travel in insulated and refrigerated semi-trailers with valid ATP certificates and up-to-date inspections.

  • Loading slots kept

    We arrive at your dock at the agreed time and warn you early about any change, so the warehouse never waits for the truck.

  • Full loads and groupage

    A full truck of 33 euro pallets or space in a groupage load for a few pallets, on routes in Romania and across Europe.

  • Romania – Italy and Europe

    Regular trips between Romania and Italy and transport across the European Union, both outbound and return.

  • GPS tracking

    You know where the goods are and at what temperature they travel. Our dispatcher sends you the truck position whenever you ask.

Why controlled temperature matters

A few degrees too warm on the road shortens the shelf life of your goods. From loading to delivery the trailer holds the set range and the temperature is logged, so you can show anyone that the cold chain was never broken.

And freight forwarding

A trip we have no free truck for, a new route or goods that need a different trailer? We organise it for you.

  • We find the right carrier and check the licence, CMR insurance and trailer.
  • We negotiate the price and keep you posted from loading to delivery.
  • You get one invoice and one contact person.
About freight forwarding

Transit warehouse in Italy

On the Romania – Italy route we use the Translusia transit warehouse in Oppeano, near Verona.

Here goods can be unloaded at the dock, sorted on pallets and kept in the cold store until delivery in Italy or until the return trip to Romania. For you, that means faster deliveries in northern Italy and groupage loads consolidated in one place.
