Types of refrigerated transport

Dual-temperature refrigerated transport

A movable bulkhead splits the trailer into two compartments, each with its own temperature. Frozen and chilled goods travel on the same trip, for example ice cream at −20 °C and dairy at +4 °C, so you do not pay for two trucks.

  • Two independent zones, between −25 °C and +25 °C
  • The bulkhead moves to fit the pallets in each zone
  • Each zone’s temperature logged separately

Groupage refrigerated transport

One pallet, five or fifteen? You pay only for the space you use. We combine several loads on the same route and deliver each one to its consignee, chilled or frozen, in standard or express transit.

  • From a single euro pallet to a full truck
  • Regular Romania – Italy routes, outbound and return
  • Chilled or frozen goods

Hanging meat transport on hooks

For chilled hanging meat we use trailers with rails and hooks fitted to the roof. Carcasses hang freely, do not touch each other and keep their shape on the road, while cold air flows around every piece.

  • Pork, beef, lamb and mutton carcasses, hams
  • Trailer washed and sanitised before every loading
  • Between −2 °C and +4 °C for the whole trip

Why controlled temperature matters

Temperature-sensitive products need precise logistics. A few degrees too warm shortens their shelf life, and a warehouse that receives warm goods rejects them. The trailer holds the set range from loading to delivery.

GoodsTrailer temperature

  • Fresh fruit and vegetables

    Tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, citrus and seasonal fruit, on pallets or in crates, straight from the grower or from the warehouse.

    Temperature: +2 °C … +12 °C

  • Dairy and cheese

    Milk, yoghurt, aged and fresh cheese for distribution centres and retail chains.

    Temperature: +2 °C … +6 °C

  • Meat and fish

    Chilled meat, including carcasses hanging on hooks, and fresh fish, in a trailer cleaned and pre-cooled before loading.

    Temperature: −2 °C … +4 °C

  • Frozen products

    Frozen vegetables and fruit, meat, ice cream and frozen bakery products.

    Temperature: −25 °C … −18 °C

  • Flowers and plants

    Cut flowers, seedlings and ornamental plants, at a steady temperature and humidity.

    Temperature: +2 °C … +10 °C

  • Other sensitive goods

    Chocolate, sweets, drinks and cosmetics that cannot take the summer heat or the winter cold.

    Temperature: +15 °C … +25 °C

Indicative ranges. We set the exact temperature from the product sheet or the receiving warehouse’s requirements.

Compliant with transport rules

International transport of perishable foodstuffs follows the ATP agreement (Agreement on the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs). Among other things, it sets:

  • the permitted temperatures for each category of goods;
  • the technical requirements for insulated bodies and refrigeration units;
  • regular inspection and calibration of the equipment.
