About Frigo Speed Italia

Refrigerated carrier and freight forwarder from Târnăveni, Mureș County, Romania.

Home/About Frigo Speed Italia

Who we are

Frigo Speed Italia carries goods that have to be kept cold: fruit and vegetables, dairy, meat and frozen products.

Our head office and truck base are in Târnăveni, on Industriei Street. From here our trucks leave for warehouses across Romania and for Italy, and our dispatcher follows every truck from loading to delivery.

Our managing director, Ioan Puczi, also runs Cona Frut România, which imports fruit and vegetables from Almería, and Dallavallero & Sergio, a carrier on the Romania – Italy route. That is why we know perishable goods from both ends of the road: as a carrier and as a customer.
