Freight forwarding

We organise your transport from request to delivery, with our own trucks or with vetted carriers.

Home/Freight forwarding

One contact person, any truck

Freight forwarding is the part of our company that does not stop at our own trucks. We take your request, find the right transport, negotiate the price and follow the trip until delivery.

You talk to a single dispatcher and receive a single invoice, even when your goods travel with a partner. Responsibility for organising the transport stays with us.

What we organise

  • Refrigerated transport through partners

    When our trucks are on the road, we put your trip on a vetted refrigerated carrier, with the same temperature conditions.

  • General cargo and curtainsiders

    Pallets, materials and equipment that need no cooling, on 13.6 m curtainsiders or vans.

  • Groupage

    A few pallets combined with other loads on the same route, so you pay only for the space you use.

  • Urgent trips

    Goods that must leave today: we look for the free truck closest to your loading dock.

How we vet our carriers

We do not hand your goods to anyone with a free truck. Before the first trip with a partner, we check:

  1. the transport licence and the certified copy for the truck
  2. CMR insurance, with a limit that matches the value of the goods
  3. the trailer’s ATP certificate, for food transport
  4. the carrier’s track record and references from other customers
